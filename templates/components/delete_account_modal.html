Delete Swimmer Account
Permanent and irreversible action
What happens when you delete your account:
  • Your personal account, STPS ID card, saved preferences, and login credentials will be permanently erased.
  • If you register or log in again in the future, you will start completely fresh as a new swimmer.
  • Completed coaching sessions and payment records remain safely archived for your coach's earnings and financial records under your swimmer name.
Active Bookings Guard: Deletion is automatically blocked if you currently have an ongoing package with remaining sessions.
